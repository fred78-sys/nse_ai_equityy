6 · Data & caveats

  • SYNTHETIC market (signal=none/placebo) -- not real NSE data
  • No fundamentals file: value/quality/growth features inactive.
  • No sentiment file: news-sentiment feature inactive.
  • Research tool, not investment advice. No method can guarantee returns; thin NSE liquidity, price limits, T+3 settlement and 15% capital-gains/withholding taxes are only roughly captured.
  • Delisted/suspended stocks are missing from most free data feeds (survivorship bias flatters backtests). Treat results as an upper bound.
  • Costs are an assumption (150 bps one-way); confirm with your broker. Dividends only count if your prices are dividend-adjusted.
